BoG policy rate

From /economic-calendar and /economic-calendar/policy-rate. Policy rate decisions are the Bank of Ghana's (Policy Rate Trends); coming MPC meetings are from BoG's published meeting calendar. Statistical releases are the Ghana Statistical Service's release calendar, which gives the week of the month only and calls its dates provisional: a release shows as its week (days 1–7, 8–14, ...), and as overdue once that week has passed without GSS marking it released. Auction dates are the Friday before issue; the next one is estimated.
