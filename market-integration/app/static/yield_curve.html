Closing yields against tenor (years to maturity, log scale). The line joins the bills; bonds are points, since each trades at its own spread. The dashed line and hollow markers are the comparison session. Old GoG bonds trade rarely and their closes are often stale, so they are off by default; GFSF bonds (structured) and USD DDE bonds (a different currency) are left out. Hover a point for the bills or bond behind it; click to open it.
