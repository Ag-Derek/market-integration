Auction rates are the Bank of Ghana's (Treasury Bill Rates), from /auctions/monitor and /auctions/results: issue date, tender, discount rate and interest rate (the yield; for notes and bonds BoG gives the coupon). Amounts offered, bid and accepted are only in BoG press releases, so they aren't here. A new bill is matched to its GFIM security by tenor and maturity date; "since auction" is its GFIM closing yield less the auction yield, so a positive number means yields have risen (prices fallen) since. The next auction is estimated from the weekly pattern (Friday auction, Monday issue, moved round holidays), not read from BoG's issuance calendar.
