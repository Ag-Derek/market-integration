Reference rates are the Bank of Ghana's interbank fix (Historical Interbank FX Rates), from /fx/rates, published once a business day. BoG quotes every currency against the cedi, so cross rates (/fx/cross) go through it: units of B per A = (GH₵ per A) ÷ (GH₵ per B), at the mid. Synthetic forwards (/fx/forwards) are what covered interest parity implies, not dealer quotes: F = S × (1 + rGH₵·d/364) ÷ (1 + rforeign·d/basis). The cedi rate is the latest 91, 182 and 364-day T-bill auction yield, interpolated by days and held flat outside that range. The foreign rate is the overnight benchmark (SOFR, €STR or SONIA) held flat for the whole tenor. Value dates run from the fix date and ignore spot lag and holidays. Points are outright minus spot in pips (0.0001). The premium is the cedi depreciation the forward implies, annualised on 365 days.
