        What moves the fee (the Greeks)

          Exchange rates are the Bank of Ghana's interbank mid, so a bank's own rate will differ by its spread. The locked-in rate is the forward implied by cedi T-bill yields and the foreign overnight rate (as on the FX page). The protection fee is estimated with the standard currency-option model (Garman-Kohlhagen, via QuantLib), using how much the rate has actually moved recently. Ghana has no traded options market, so treat it as a guide to what's fair, not a price you can deal at. Bank fees and spreads aren't included.
