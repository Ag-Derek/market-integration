Live over /ws/market, the same feed the rest of the pipeline uses. Prices follow the GSE: the headline price is the session's volume-weighted average (the GSE closing price) and the change is measured against the previous VWAP close; the chart plots individual trades. The status badge reflects the trading session and the feed's connection (/market/status), never price direction. Market cap is the price times shares outstanding, shown as — until shares outstanding are recorded for the company. The cards are your watchlist, saved for this browser: pin from the search bar, unpin with ✕, drag ⠿ to reorder (/watchlists/me).
