Quote over /ws/market; chart from /candles?symbol=…&range=…, with up to 200 earlier candles (&warmup=) so indicators have a value from the first candle shown. History is backfilled by the mock connector on startup and extended live as ticks arrive. As on the GSE, the headline price is the session's volume-weighted average (the closing price) and its change is against the previous VWAP close; the chart plots individual trades. The status badge reflects the trading session and the feed's connection (/market/status), never price direction. Market cap is the price times shares outstanding, shown as — until shares outstanding are recorded for the company. Beta, PE, EPS, dividends and the target here are still generated placeholders; the calculated versions are on the Description view (/instruments/{symbol}/description). In another currency (top right), the quote is converted at the latest Bank of Ghana interbank mid-rate (/fx/rates), so its percentage change is the shares' own; daily and weekly charts use each day's rate (/candles?…&currency=…), so they show what the shares were worth in that currency at the time.
