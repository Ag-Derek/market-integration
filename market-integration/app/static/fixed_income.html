Live over /ws/market; the day's report is /fixed-income/report. Government securities are quoted by yield with the clean price derived from it; corporates by price only. Closing yields follow the GFIM end-of-day methodology, so they can sit outside the day's traded range. Change is the close against the open, in basis points of yield (price points for corporates). A row flashes green when its price rises (its yield falls) and red when it falls. Sell/buy-backs are repos on GoG bonds, shown apart from outright trades.
