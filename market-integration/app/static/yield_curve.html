Government of Ghana yield curve (GHS)

Loading the curve…

Closing yields against tenor (years to maturity, log scale). The solid line is a Nelson-Siegel-Svensson curve fitted to the bills, New GoG and 2023 DDEP bonds (with it off, the line joins the bills); bonds are points, since each trades at its own spread. Corporates (triangles) are placed at a yield worked out from their price; one above the chart is pinned to its top edge. The dashed line and hollow markers are the comparison session. Old GoG bonds trade rarely and their closes are often stale, so they are off by default; GFSF bonds (structured) and USD DDE bonds (a different currency) are left out. Hover a point for the bills or bond behind it; click to open it.

    From /curve?date=YYYY-MM-DD and /spreads?date=YYYY-MM-DD, live over /ws/market for the current session. A date without a session (a weekend or holiday) shows the session before it.
