Monthly averages from the World Bank's Pink Sheet, via /commodities/prices, in nominal US dollars per the unit shown (cocoa, coffee, rubber, cotton and sugar per kg, as the World Bank quotes them). It is updated in the first week of each month with the month before. Cedi prices are the US$ average times the average of that month's Bank of Ghana USD mid fixes, so the cedi change year on year includes the cedi's own move. These are international benchmarks, not prices realised in Ghana. Each name opens its history on the Graph tab; /commodities/{code}/history has it as data.
