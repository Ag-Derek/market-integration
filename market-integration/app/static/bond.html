The quote header updates live. The calculator follows the closing yield until you type a price or yield, or pick the bid or ask. It settles T+2 on the GFIM calendar by default, so its price can differ a little from the quoted price, which is valued at the trade date. Bills are simple interest on ACT/364. GoG bonds pay semi-annual coupons, ACT/ACT, with yields compounded semi-annually (see docs/fixed-income-sources-and-conventions.md). DV01 is the price change for a 1bp fall in yield. OAS, Z-spread and after-tax yield aren't covered yet. Corporate, GFSF and USD DDE bonds need term sheets before they can be priced.
